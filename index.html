PDF tables into spreadsheets you can check.

Convert PDF tables to Excel free in your browser. PDFs with a text layer are read exactly; scans, photos and screenshots are read with OCR or AI. Every total is checked against its rows, and you can edit any cell before you download.

Convert PDF to Excel without retyping

PDFCells turns tables in PDF files into Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx). It handles bank and credit card statements, invoices, financial reports, price lists, scanned paperwork, photos and screenshots. Open a PDF or image and every page is checked first: text pages are read exactly from the PDF text, scanned pages with OCR, and broken or irregular pages can go to AI. Before you download, every table opens in an editable grid next to the page, with cells that need a look flagged.

PDFCells is open source under the MIT licence. The code on GitHub shows what runs in your browser and what is sent when you choose AI, and you can check it in your browser too.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDFCells free?

Yes. Reading the PDF text and running OCR on scans are free, with no page quota, because both run in your browser. Signed-in users also get 25 free AI pages a day on Fast. After that, AI pages cost Anthropic's token price plus 15%, usually well under a cent a page on Fast. You can also use your own Anthropic API key.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is opened and converted on your device. Nothing leaves the browser unless you choose an AI feature: reading a page with AI sends that page as an image with its text, categorising with AI sends payee names, and naming tables sends table headers and a few rows. Our server passes them to Anthropic and does not store them.

Can it convert scanned PDFs to Excel?

Yes. Every page is checked first. Scanned pages can be read with free OCR in your browser, or with AI (Claude), which handles skewed, low-quality and photographed pages better.

Can I convert a photo or screenshot of a table?

Yes. Open a photo or screenshot (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC from an iPhone, or TIFF), or paste a screenshot straight into the page. Several images open together as one document, one page each. Image pages are read with free OCR in your browser, or with AI (Claude).

How do I fix mistakes before downloading?

Every table opens in an editable grid next to the page. Cells that look wrong are flagged: totals that don't add up, text in a number column, or cells OCR was unsure of. You can edit cells, merge or split columns, or ask AI to fix a table in plain English.

Does it keep numbers as numbers in Excel?

Yes. Amounts, percentages, negatives in parentheses, currencies, decimal-comma formats and dates become real Excel values with consistent column formats. Header rows are frozen, and tables that continue across pages are joined into one sheet.
