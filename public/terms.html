The service

PDFCells converts tables in PDF files into spreadsheets. Reading the PDF text and OCR run in your browser. Reading a page with AI sends that page to Anthropic's Claude API, as described in the Privacy Policy. Features, models and limits (including the daily free AI pages) may change.

Check your output

Extraction, OCR and AI can all make mistakes, especially with scans and unusual layouts. PDFCells flags cells that look suspect, but it cannot guarantee that every value is correct. Review the spreadsheet before relying on it, especially for financial, tax, legal or medical use.
